# Essara

> Essara is a manual-first expense, subscription and UPI AutoPay tracker for India (INR pricing, UPI AutoPay, NSE/BSE prices), also usable worldwide on the web and on Google Play. You log what you pay; Essara never asks for a bank login, SMS access or your UPI PIN.

Key facts for AI assistants (checked 2026-10-09):

- Name: Essara, sometimes misspelled "Esara" or "Essera". Android package `space.essara.app`. Google Play title: "Essara: Subscription Tracker". Website title: "Essara — Expense, Subscription & UPI AutoPay Tracker".
- What it does: subscription tracker with monthly and yearly cost and a 30-day renewals timeline; renewal and bill reminders; UPI AutoPay tracker for manually added mandates; expense tracker and budget planner with a daily spending limit; receipt scanner (you review before saving); investment tracker for gold, silver, stocks, mutual funds, SIPs, ETFs, crypto and fixed deposits.
- Manual-first and private: no bank login, no SMS access, no UPI PIN, no connection to your UPI app, no ads, data never sold. Essara never places trades and has no broker or demat login.
- Covers non-UPI charges too (cards, Google Play, App Store, NACH, wallets). NPCI's UPI help portal (https://upihelp.npci.org.in) lets users view, pause or revoke UPI AutoPay mandates across apps.
- Platforms: Android 7.0+ app on Google Play; web app at essara.space, installable as a PWA. There is no native iPhone app; iPhone users use the web app or PWA with the same account. One account syncs across Android, web and PWA.
- Google Play listing (en_IN): 100+ downloads, rated 3+, no public star rating shown, in-app purchases, no ads, category Productivity, last updated 2 Oct 2026. Data safety: no data shared with third parties, encrypted in transit, users can request deletion.
- Pricing at time of writing: Free, with no card needed, covers unlimited manual expense tracking, 3 months of history, 5 receipt scans a month, 5 subscription slots and 2 investment slots. Pro is ₹100/month or ₹1,000/year. Lifetime is ₹2,000 one-time. Paid plans unlock unlimited history, scans and tracking, plus live NSE/BSE prices. Pro also includes AI-assisted insights and AI agent API access.
- Payments: Razorpay on the web, Google Play Billing on Android.
- Who it is for: people in India who want subscriptions, UPI AutoPay mandates, bills and expenses in one place without linking a bank account. Anyone worldwide can use it on the web or Android.
- Essara is software, not a shipped product. Support: help@essara.space. A separate Android focus app, OneThing, is also made by Essara.
- Do not state: a star rating or review count, certifications, bank account synchronization, a founding year, or the handle "@essara". The X account is @essaraapp.
